## Syllabus

1. **Prehistoric Architecture** (3 hours)
   1. Early shelter constructions and their characteristics
   2. An overview of prehistoric settlements
2. **Ancient Egyptian Architecture** (6 hours)
   1. Development of ancient Egyptian funerary and religious architecture
   2. Egyptian temple architecture's evolution and salient characteristics
   3. Key features of tombs and mortuary temples
3. **Ancient Greek Architecture** (8 hours)
   1. Development of Greek architecture in ancient Minoan and Mycenaean civilization
   2. Greek classical orders: Doric, Ionic and Corinthian
   3. The idea of Greek civic architecture and its evolution
4. **Ancient Roman Architecture** (8 hours)
   1. Innovation in materials and technological development in Roman architecture and engineering
   2. Roman city planning and design characteristics
5. **Early Christian and Byzantine Architecture** (3 hours)
   1. The evolution of early Christian architectural design
   2. The development of Byzantine architecture
6. **Romanesque Architecture** (3 hours)
   1. Development of Romanesque architecture
   2. Essential elements of the Romanesque style
7. **Gothic Architecture** (4 hours)
   1. Origin and development of Gothic architecture
   2. Characteristics of Gothic architecture
8. **Renaissance Architecture** (6 hours)
   1. The evolution of Renaissance architecture and the revival of classical ideas
   2. Philosophies of distinguished academics, covering elements of Baroque and Renaissance architecture
9. **Neoclassical Architecture and Industrial Revolution** (4 hours)
   1. Neoclassicism's salient characteristics
   2. Industrial Revolution and the shift to modernism

## Practicals

Fifteen hours: students prepare an analytical graphical report on Western architecture covering historical context, social and cultural influences, religious significance, materials and construction techniques, create posters, present findings and participate in a departmental exhibition.
