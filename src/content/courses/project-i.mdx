## Syllabus

Under the direction of the project supervisor, each group of 2 to 4 students selects a topic related to Geomatics Engineering, including surveying, GIS, remote sensing, photogrammetry, geospatial data science, cartography, land administration, navigation systems, web GIS application or other relevant applications. In Project I students are required to complete the following works according to the category of project.

1. **Design Type Project**
   1. Background
   2. Project description
   3. Study area
   4. Literature review/guidelines
   5. Methodology
   6. Field data collection and plotting
2. **Dissertation Type Project**
   1. Background
   2. Need of the research
   3. Objectives and scope of the work
   4. Literature review
   5. Study area
   6. Methodology
   7. Data collection and compilation
3. **Experimental Type Project**
   1. Background
   2. Need of the research
   3. Objectives and scope of the work
   4. Literature Review
   5. Experimental setup
   6. Methodology
   7. Data collection and compilation

## Evaluation

1. Selection of project type and supervisor: proposal submitted within two weeks, mentioning objectives, scope of work and outcome
2. Mid-term progress presentation (4 weeks before end of term): group presentation and viva, 20 marks
3. Final presentation and report submission (last week of term): draft report reviewed before final submission, evaluated by supervisor for 30 marks
4. Full marks for Project I is 50
