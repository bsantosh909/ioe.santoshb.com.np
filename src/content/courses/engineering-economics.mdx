## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (2 hours)
   1. Micro, macro, and engineering economics (History, fundamental principles, and applications)
   2. Terminology related to engineering economics
   3. Economic decisions and the role of engineers in decision-making
   4. Cash flow and cash flow diagrams
2. **Market Economics** (3 hours)
   1. Market, demand, supply, and their relationships
   2. Elasticity, application of elasticity, and government policies
   3. Externality and market inefficiency
   4. Market failure and firm behavior
3. **Cost** (2 hours)
   1. Cost classification (Total, average, fixed, variable, marginal; Direct, indirect, standard; Cash vs. book; Sunk, opportunity, life-cycle)
   2. Cost estimation and control
4. **Time Value of Money** (6 hours)
   1. Money (Types, functions, and time value)
   2. Simple and compound interest (Nominal, effective, continuous)
   3. Economic equivalence
   4. Cash flow types (Single, uniform, linear gradient, geometric gradient, irregular)
5. **Methods of Economic Analysis** (12 hours)
   1. Capital budgeting and MARR
   2. Payback period, Equivalent worth, Rate of return, Benefit-cost analysis
   3. WACC and repeatability assumptions
   4. Multiple investment project alternatives
6. **Replacement Analysis** (5 hours)
   1. Replacement strategies and economic service life
   2. Replacement strategy for finite and infinite planning horizons
7. **Risk Analysis** (5 hours)
   1. Origin of risk in projects
   2. Sensitivity, breakeven, scenario analyses, and decision trees
8. **Depreciation and Taxes** (5 hours)
   1. Depreciation methods (Straight line, declining balance, sinking fund, sum-of-years'-digits, MACRS)
   2. Tax and after-tax cash flow analysis
9. **Measurement of National Income** (5 hours)
   1. GDP components and calculations
   2. Unemployment, inflation causes/effects
   3. Real/nominal exchange rates, fiscal budget, monetary policy
