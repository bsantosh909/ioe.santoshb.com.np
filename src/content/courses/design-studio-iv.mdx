## Syllabus

1. **Project Introduction** (10 hours)
   1. Introduction to building typologies
   2. Programmatic and functional requirements
   3. Overview of architectural and structural systems
   4. Focus on climate-responsive design
2. **Literature Review and Case Studies** (30 hours)
   1. Conduct literature review on climate-responsive design
   2. Review of relevant standards and guidelines
   3. Analyze case studies on space planning and design
   4. Extract design strategies for climate-adaptive solutions
3. **Site Analysis** (10 hours)
   1. Conduct site analysis: physical, sociocultural, environmental
   2. Identify site-specific climate conditions and influences
   3. Prepare site analysis diagrams
4. **Conceptual Design** (10 hours)
   1. Develop climate-responsive design concepts
   2. Explore massing, form and spatial organization
   3. Focus on sustainability and energy efficiency
   4. Create schematic designs
5. **Design Development** (65 hours)
   1. Application of inferences from literature and case studies
   2. Refine design into detailed drawings
   3. Focus on floor plans, sections and elevations
   4. Integrate climate-responsive and sustainable strategies
   5. Develop physical model
6. **Final Design Representation and Presentation** (12 hours)
   1. Prepare and present final drawings and models
   2. Refine presentation with climate-responsive focus
   3. Submit final drawings and 3D model
7. **Final Presentation and Defense** (7 hours)
8. **Time Problem** (6 hours)
