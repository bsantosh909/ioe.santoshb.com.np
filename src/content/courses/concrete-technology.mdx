## Syllabus

1. **Concrete and Constituents of Concrete** (6 hours)
   1. Introduction to concrete and use of concrete in structures
   2. Constituents of concrete: Aggregates, Cement, Water, Admixtures
   3. Structure of concrete as three-phase system
2. **Properties of Fresh Concrete and Concreting Operations** (4 hours)
   1. Water-cement ratio
   2. Workability and its tests
   3. Segregation, bleeding and their effects
   4. Concreting operations and procedures
   5. Concreting in extreme temperatures
   6. Mass concreting issues
3. **Mix Design of Concrete** (6 hours)
   1. Compressive strength and grade designation
   2. Variability of concrete strength
   3. Nominal mix introduction
   4. Mix design methods (IS, British, ACI codes)
4. **Properties of Hardened Concrete** (6 hours)
   1. Load-deformation behavior and elasticity
   2. Shrinkage and creep
   3. Impact, cyclic loading, and fatigue
   4. Effect of water-cement ratio and porosity
   5. Concrete maturity and durability
5. **Testing of Concrete, Quality Assurance and Control** (5 hours)
   1. Strength testing (tensile, compressive, shear, bond)
   2. Acceptance criteria
   3. Non-destructive tests
   4. Quality assurance and monitoring
6. **Special Types of Concretes** (3 hours)
   1. Lightweight, high-density, fiber-reinforced concretes
   2. Self-healing and high-performance concretes
   3. Emerging technologies

## Practicals

1. Aggregate gradation
2. Nominal mix design
3. Concrete mix design methods
4. Workability tests
5. Concrete testing
6. Non-destructive testing procedures
