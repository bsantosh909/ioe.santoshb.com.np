## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (3 hours)
   1. Historical development and evolution of UAVs
   2. UAV types and classifications
   3. Unmanned aerial system (UAS) components
   4. Key applications and industry sectors using UAVs
2. **UAV Co-ordinate System, Aerodynamics and Flight Mechanics** (5 hours)
   1. UAV co-ordinate system
   2. Lift, drag, thrust and weight considerations
   3. Basic principles of aeromechanics
   4. Stability and control in fixed-wing and quadrotor UAVs
   5. Flight dynamics: pitch, roll, yaw and their control
3. **Fixed-Wing UAV Design Principles** (10 hours)
   1. Structural components and materials
   2. Wing configurations and their impact on flight performance
   3. Propulsion systems: types of engines and propellers
   4. Flight envelope and performance characteristics
   5. UAV design process
4. **Quadrotor UAV Design Principles** (8 hours)
   1. Basic quadrotor design and layout
   2. Motor and propeller selection
   3. Frame design and materials
   4. Power systems and energy management
   5. Payload integration
   6. Mathematical modelling of quadcopter
   7. PID control of quadcopter
5. **UAV Avionics and Sensors** (6 hours)
   1. Overview of avionics systems in UAVs
   2. Key sensors: IMUs, GPS, barometers, cameras, optical flow and LIDAR
   3. Sensor fusion and estimation (extended Kalman filter)
   4. Data communication and telemetry systems
   5. Integration of sensors for autonomous flight
6. **Autonomous Navigation and Control Systems** (8 hours)
   1. Introduction to flight control systems (FCS)
   2. UAS autonomy level
   3. Autonomous navigation algorithms: waypoint navigation, path planning (A star)
   4. Obstacle detection and avoidance systems
   5. GPS denied navigation: visual inertial odometry, SLAM, computer vision
7. **UAV Applications and Regulations** (5 hours)
   1. UAV regulations and airspace management (CAAN, ICAO)
   2. Overview of commercial and military UAV applications
   3. Ethical considerations in UAV operations
   4. Future trends in UAV technology and industry outlook

## Practicals

1. Conceptual design and analysis of UAV
2. Application of PID controllers and tuning
3. Fabrication of a quadcopter or fixed-wing UAV using 3D printing
4. Automation integration in SITL and real flight
