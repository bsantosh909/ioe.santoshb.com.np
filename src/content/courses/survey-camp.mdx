## Syllabus

1. **Establishment of Horizontal Control for Major Traverse** (2 days)
   1. Reconnaissance, stations selection and pegging of closed traverse (1.5 km or more perimeter, 15-20 stations) controlled with national grid reference
   2. Measurement by Total Station/DGPS
   3. Level transfer using Auto level/DGPS
   4. Coordinate computation (NEZ)
2. **Minor Traverse and Topographic Survey** (5 days)
   1. Link traverse (5-7 stations) controlled to major traverse
   2. Angle and distance measurement by Total Station/DGPS
   3. Level transfer and coordinate computation
   4. Plotting of both traverses
   5. Detailed topographic survey from major and minor control points: Semi built up area around 4 to 6 hectares of land using Total Station/Drone
   6. CAD digital data recording and plotting
3. **Bridge Site Survey** (1.5 days)
   1. Detailed topographic survey of suitable bridge site area (at least 200m by 120m)
   2. Topographic map, L-section and X-section preparation
4. **Road Alignment Survey** (1.5 days)
   1. Topographic survey of road alignment (corridor at least 650m by 30m)
   2. Plan, L-section and X-section with Right of Way 20m preparation
