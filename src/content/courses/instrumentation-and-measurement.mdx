## Syllabus

1. **Instrumentation Systems** (2 hours)
   1. Functional components of instrumentation systems
   2. Signal processing, transmission, and output indication
   3. Electrical, electronics, pneumatic, and hydraulic working media systems
   4. Conversion devices
   5. Analog and digital systems
2. **Theory of Measurement** (8 hours)
   1. Static performance parameters: accuracy, precision, sensitivity, resolution, linearity
   2. Dynamic performance parameters: response time, frequency response, bandwidth
   3. Error analysis and statistical methods
   4. Moving coil and moving iron instruments for voltage/current measurement
   5. Low, high, and medium resistance measurement
   6. AC bridges for inductance and capacitance measurement
3. **Transducers** (10 hours)
   1. Classification and applications
   2. Mechanical variable measurement: displacement, strain, velocity, acceleration, vibration
   3. Process variable measurement: temperature, pressure, level, flow, pH, humidity
   4. Biophysical variable measurement: blood pressure, myoelectric potentials
4. **Electrical Signal Processing and Transmission** (6 hours)
   1. Op-amp characteristics and instrumentation amplifiers
   2. Signal amplification, attenuation, integration, differentiation
   3. Noise effects and analog/digital filtering
   4. Optical communication and fiber optics
5. **Analog-Digital and Digital-Analog Conversion** (6 hours)
   1. DACs: weighted resistor and R-2R ladder types with error analysis
   2. ADCs: successive approximation, ramp, dual ramp, flash types with error analysis
6. **Digital Instrumentation** (6 hours)
   1. Sample-and-hold circuits
   2. Data acquisition system components
   3. Computer interfacing
7. **Electrical Equipment** (7 hours)
   1. Wattmeters, energy meters, frequency meters
   2. Power factor meters
   3. Instrument transformers

## Practicals

1. Meter accuracy testing
2. Operational amplifier applications
3. Transducer measurements
4. ADC/DAC converter testing
