## Syllabus

1. **First Studio Project** (6 hours)
   1. Project briefing
   2. Literature review
   3. Case studies selection
   4. Field research method
   5. Site briefing
2. **Literature and Case Study** (20 hours)
   1. Library study on standards
   2. Online study on international cases
   3. On site study of cases
   4. Stakeholder interaction
   5. Field data review
   6. Site visit and site drawings
   7. Literature, case study and site analysis presentation
3. **Conceptualization** (10 hours)
   1. Program formulation
   2. Bubble diagram
   3. Conceptual design in 2D and 3D
   4. Presentation and feedback
4. **Design Consultation and Refinement** (36 hours)
   1. Design concept development
   2. Single line master plan
   3. Block model
   4. Consultation and refinement
   5. Double line floor plans with furniture layout
   6. Presentation on architecture drawings and block model
5. **Final Presentation and Submission of First Studio Project** (15 hours)
   1. Preparation for submission
   2. Submission of detail architecture drawings and detail 3D model
   3. Final presentation and external jury feedback
6. **Second Studio Project** (8 hours)
   1. Project briefing and site brief
   2. Literature review and case study
   3. Site analysis
7. **Design Concept and Design Development** (30 hours)
   1. Program formulation, bubble diagram and zoning
   2. Conceptual design, drawing and block model
   3. Consultation and improvisation in design
   4. Presentation and feedback
8. **Final Presentation and Submission of Second Studio Project** (12 hours)
   1. Preparation for submission
   2. Submission of detail architecture drawings and detail 3D model
9. **Final Presentation and Defense** (7 hours)
10. **Time Problem** (6 hours)
    1. Designing a specified facility in specific given time
    2. Submission of conceptual and architecture drawings and 3D sketches
