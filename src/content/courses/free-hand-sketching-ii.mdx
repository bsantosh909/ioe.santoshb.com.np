## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (4 hours)
   1. Importance of free hand sketching in architecture
   2. Advance free hand sketching skill: interior, exterior, landscaping and city scaping
   3. Warm up exercises of light, shade and shadow using pencil, ink and colour
   4. Knowledge of architectural rendering
2. **Free Hand Practice of Landscaping** (4 hours)
   1. Free hand exercise of landscaping by colour techniques
   2. Free hand exercise of landscaping by ink techniques
3. **Fast Sketching** (4 hours)
   1. Fast sketching of 3D forms: trees, vehicles, furniture, human figures in colour and ink
   2. Free hand sketching of self-portrait using pencil and colour techniques
4. **Free Hand Sketching of Interior Spaces** (6 hours)
   1. Interior of historical spaces with rendering in pencil, ink and colour
   2. Interior of modern spaces with rendering in pencil, ink and colour
5. **Free Hand Sketching of Exterior Spaces** (6 hours)
   1. Exterior of historical spaces with rendering in pencil, ink and colour
   2. Exterior of modern spaces with rendering in pencil, ink and colour
   3. City scaping in mixed medium
6. **Sketching by Memory and Rendering in Building and Surrounding** (6 hours)
   1. Sketching of building and environment by memory in pencil
   2. Rendering on facade and 3D views of building and surroundings in colour and ink medium

## Practicals

Forty-five hours of exercises covering line and tones in water colour and ink pen, landscaping, fast sketching of 3D forms, self-portrait, interior and exterior of modern and historical spaces, sketching by memory, rendering of facades and 3D views, city scaping, and a test on rendering.
