## Syllabus

1. **Steel Structure** (4 hours)
   1. Shapes of structural steel, types of connection in steel works (riveting, bolting, welding)
   2. Types of steel structural members: beams, columns and girders
   3. Details at foundation level, beam and column connection
2. **Roof Trusses** (4 hours)
   1. Timber and steel trusses, types and details
   2. Flat and pitched trusses and their details
3. **Reinforced Cement Concrete Floors** (4 hours)
   1. Introduction to floors
   2. Types of floor, upper framed and self-centering floors
   3. Typical details
4. **Concrete Staircase** (4 hours)
   1. Precast and cast-in-situ concrete staircase
   2. Types, construction and details
5. **Joints in Concrete** (4 hours)
   1. Joints in concrete: construction, expansion, contraction, isolation and sliding joints
   2. Location and spacing of joints for slabs, beams, columns, walls
   3. Bonding of new to old concrete
   4. Materials used in expansion joints
6. **Claddings** (4 hours)
   1. Cladding for external and internal finishes, necessities and usage
   2. Cladding types and detailing
   3. Plasterwork, paints, dry lining, tiles, quarry tiles and internal fixings
7. **False Ceiling** (2 hours)
   1. Types and usage of false ceiling
   2. Materials and construction technology
8. **Cavity Walls and Partitions** (4 hours)
   1. Cavity walls: purpose, advantages and disadvantages
   2. Curtain walls, materials used and concept of load transference
   3. Lightweight partitions, materials used and requirements
   4. Steel framed partitions

## Practicals

Forty-five hours preparing drawings and detailing of steel structures, roof trusses, concrete floors, concrete staircases (cast-in-situ and precast), joints in concrete, cladding, false ceiling, and walls and partitions.
