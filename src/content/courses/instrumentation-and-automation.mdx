## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (7 hours)
   1. Functional elements of measurement system and instrument system
   2. Classification of instruments: Deflection and null types, manually operated and automatic types, analog and digital types; Self-generating and power-operated types, contacting and non-contacting types, dumb and intelligent types; Application of instrument systems
   3. Standard and calibration
2. **Characteristics of Instruments** (6 hours)
   1. Types of errors and uncertainties in instrumentations
   2. Static performance parameters: Accuracy, repeatability, precision, threshold, sensitivity, resolution, linearity, range and span, hysteresis, dead band, drift, backlash, selection of instrument
   3. Dynamic response: Periodic input-harmonic type, general periodic input – non-harmonic type, transient type input and random input; compensation
3. **Process Instrumentation** (9 hours)
   1. Working principles of instruments used for the measurement of flow, level, force, pressure, temperature, density, viscosity, humidity, pH value, turbidity
   2. Advantage and disadvantage of instruments
   3. Application considerations
4. **Building Blocks of Instrument** (8 hours)
   1. Classification, principles and applications of sensors and transducers
   2. Transducer, amplifier, signal, conditioner
   3. Signal isolation, signal transmitter, display
   4. Data acquisition modules, I/O devices, signal convertors, interfaces
5. **Control System and Automation Strategy** (6 hours)
   1. Evolution of instrumentation and control
   2. Types of industries and automation
   3. Role of automation in industries
   4. Benefits of automation and automation strategy evolution
6. **Computer Based Control Systems** (9 hours)
   1. Introduction, evolution, types, architecture and applications of PLC system
   2. Introduction, evolution, architecture, types and applications of DCS system
   3. Introduction, evolution, types, architecture and applications of SCADA system
