## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (5 hours)
   1. Concept and evolution of human settlements
   2. Growth and decline of settlements, planning objectives
   3. Rural, urban, peri-urban and informal settlements
   4. Global, South Asian and Nepali contexts
   5. Settlement structure (urban and rural)
   6. Socio-spatial systems (spatial form, social structure and economic process)
2. **Classical Settlement-Oriented Thoughts and Approaches** (10 hours)
   1. Settlement morphology: form, pattern, structure, transformation
   2. Utopian planning: Howard, Geddes, Perry, Doxiadis, Mumford
   3. Modernist planning: Le Corbusier and Frank Lloyd Wright
   4. Conceptual perspectives on settlements: production and use of space, informality, land, economy and institutions
3. **Settlement Planning Scales, Tools and Techniques** (8 hours)
   1. Overview of planning approaches (post 1950s)
   2. Settlement planning scales: national to local
   3. Settlement planning components: land use planning, development control, planning regulations and building by-laws
   4. Concept of spatial analysis: spatial data and thematic mapping, density and land-use distribution, role of mapping
4. **Rural Settlement Planning** (6 hours)
   1. Rural settlement concepts
   2. Physical planning in rural areas
   3. Infrastructure and service systems
   4. Public and community spaces
   5. Rural-urban linkages
   6. Traditional rural settlements
   7. Rural transformation (migration, livelihood systems, rural urban continuum)
5. **Settlement Analysis and Profiling** (8 hours)
   1. Base mapping and field reconnaissance
   2. Stakeholder identification and mapping
   3. Data collection methods: quantitative, qualitative, field observation
   4. Sectoral data analysis, descriptive statistics and SWOT analysis
6. **Emerging Directions in Settlement Planning** (8 hours)
   1. Global agenda (SDG 11, Sendai Framework, Paris Agreement, New Urban Agenda)
   2. Climate and disaster risk
   3. Ecological planning and nature-based solutions
   4. Inclusive and gender responsive settlements
   5. Low carbon and resource-efficient settlements
   6. Concept of resilient settlements

## Practicals

Assignments on case studies of rural and urban settlements in Nepal, planning problems and solutions through classical and contemporary lenses, and settlement profiling covering mapping, land-use structure, socio-economic characteristics and infrastructure services.
