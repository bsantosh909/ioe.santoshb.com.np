## Syllabus

1. **Introduction to the Field of Architecture** (2 hours)
   1. General definition of architecture, definitions by pioneer and master architects
   2. Architectural education in Nepal
2. **Architecture, Built Environment and the Society** (6 hours)
   1. Built environment: definition, internal and external built environment, three concepts of Siegfried Gideon
   2. Building and society: buildings evolution as social needs, values and society
   3. Architecture and its societal objectivity and responsibility, interpretation (semantics) and role in achieving societal purpose
3. **Social, Cultural and Religious Context of Architecture** (5 hours)
   1. Social context: social influences in architectural development
   2. Religious context: architectural development based on religious beliefs (temples, churches, mosques, pyramids)
   3. Cultural context: notions of cultural accretions and architectural development
4. **Technology and Material Context of Architecture** (3 hours)
   1. Technology and material use in different periods of architecture, prehistoric to contemporary
   2. Technology and material use in traditional Nepalese (Newar) architecture
5. **Site, City and Ecological Context of Architecture** (6 hours)
   1. Site and its nature: site as determinants, socio-cultural factors
   2. Site, environment and ecology: religious, symbiotic and exploitative relationships
   3. Introduction to sustainable design, ecological sustainability and green building design
6. **Architecture and Public Spaces** (2 hours)
7. **Architecture and its Relationship with Allied Professionals** (1 hour)
8. **Role and Relation of Architect, Client and Contractor** (1 hour)
9. **The Profession of Architecture in Nepal** (1 hour)
   1. Career opportunities in public and private sector
10. **Planning and Building Controls, Bye-laws** (3 hours)
    1. Land use planning and zoning
    2. Building bye-laws: GCR, FAR, set back, ROW, light plane
    3. National Building Code
