## Syllabus

1. **Operational Amplifier Characterization** (9 hours)
   1. Input offset voltage
   2. Input bias and input offset currents
   3. Output impedance
   4. Differential and common-mode input impedance
   5. DC gain, bandwidth, gain-bandwidth product
   6. Common-mode and power supply rejection ratio
   7. Higher frequency poles settling time
   8. Slew rate
   9. Noise in operational amplifier circuits
2. **Digital-to-Analog and Analog-to-Digital Conversion** (7 hours)
   1. Performance parameters of DAC and ADC
   2. Binary weighted resistor DAC
   3. The R-2R ladder DAC
   4. Unipolar and bipolar D/A converters
   5. Count-up and tracking A/D's based on D/A's
   6. Successive approximation A/D converters
   7. Integrating voltage-to-time conversion A/D converters, dual and quad slope types
   8. Sigma delta A/D converters
   9. Flash A/D converters
3. **Instrumentation and Isolation Amplifiers** (4 hours)
   1. One and two operational amplifier instrumentation amplifiers
   2. The three operational amplifier instrumentation amplifier
   3. Consideration of non-ideal properties
   4. Isolation amplifier principles and realization
   5. Consideration of non-ideal properties
4. **Operational Amplifier-Bipolar Transistor Logarithmic Amplifier** (4 hours)
   1. The basic logarithmic amplifier
   2. Non-ideal effects
   3. Stability consideration
   4. Anti-logarithmic operations
5. **Log-Antilog Circuit Application** (4 hours)
   1. Analog multiplier based on log-antilog principles
   2. The multifunction converter circuit
   3. Proportional to absolute temperature (PTAT) devices
   4. RMS to DC conversion
6. **Power Electronics** (11 hours)
   1. Power diodes, power MOSFET V-I characteristics
   2. Thyristor: V-I, turn on-off mechanism, protection schemes, firing circuits, series/parallel combination, self and forced commutation
   3. Members of thyristor family: DIAC, TRIAC
   4. Controlled rectifier circuits: single phase half wave and full wave with RLE load
   5. Inverters: Fixed voltage variable frequency (half and full), variable voltage variable frequency (PWM inverters)
   6. Choppers: Principle of operation, control strategies, step up chopper, classification of chopper circuits
7. **Switched Mode Power Supplies and Drives** (6 hours)
   1. Switch mode power supply
   2. Buck, boost, buck-boost regulator
   3. DC drives: classification and choice of selection
   4. Review of characteristics and operating modes of DC motors
   5. Half wave and full wave single phase converters drives
   6. Stepper motor and drives

## Practicals

1. Characteristics of Operational amplifier
2. 4-bit D to A converter
3. Differential and Instrumentation amplifiers
4. Logarithmic amplifiers, multipliers, RMS to DC conversion
5. Switched voltage regulator design
6. Silicon-controlled-rectifier (SCR) circuit design
7. Stepper Motor control with driver module and arduino
