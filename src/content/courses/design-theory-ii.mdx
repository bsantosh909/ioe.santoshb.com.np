## Syllabus

1. **Theory in Architecture** (6 hours)
   1. Basics of theory (elements and characteristics)
   2. Theory and history
   3. Theory and design
   4. Theory and criticism
   5. Theory and key architectural manifestos
2. **Space, Place and Atmosphere** (7 hours)
   1. Space as a human dimension (spatial needs)
   2. Space, distance and time
   3. Sense of place (genius loci)
   4. Atmosphere (Zumthor)
   5. Hierarchies of spaces: public and private space
3. **Meaning, Language and Symbolism in Architecture** (8 hours)
   1. Meaning: symbolic and cultural context
   2. Form language versus pattern language
   3. Basics of symbolism (signs and symbols)
   4. Examples of iconography and metaphor
   5. Case studies on meaning in architecture
4. **Context in Architecture** (8 hours)
   1. Contextualization: integration with surroundings
   2. Decontextualization: innovation and exploration
   3. Recontextualization: fitting and standing out
   4. Cultural sensitivity in context
   5. Environmental sensitivity in context
5. **Responsibilities in Architecture** (8 hours)
   1. Ecological responsibility
   2. Individual and community roles
   3. Social engagement
   4. Participatory design
   5. Case studies on ethical practices
6. **Procedural and Reflective Approaches** (8 hours)
   1. Design problem-solving techniques
   2. Scenes and storytelling
   3. Designer in action: situations, mental spaces and frames
   4. Decision parameter in design
   5. Peer feedback and self-reflection

## Practicals

Fifteen hours of tutorials: self-site visit and spatial analysis, contextual review of a building's fit with its context, group discussion on sustainability and community responsibility, and a problem-solving workshop applying design thinking to an architectural challenge.
