## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (2 hours)
   1. Basic ranging techniques: LiDAR; RADAR; Other techniques (SONAR, SLR, LADAR, VLBI)
   2. Comparison among various techniques
   3. Importance of ranging technique
2. **LiDAR** (3 hours)
   1. LiDAR and its platform
   2. LiDAR terminologies
   3. Laser scanning systems
   4. LiDAR supported file formats
3. **Airborne Laser Scanning (ALS) Technology** (8 hours)
   1. Operating principles: Electromagnetic spectrum (Light spectrum; Laser emission; Absorption and reflectance); Features of laser beam (Laser pulse characteristics; Laser pulse repetition frequency; Laser beam divergence; Laser pulse energy distribution); Laser Ranging (Laser beam, target interaction)
   2. Key elements of ALS technology: Laser, LIDAR, and working principle (Laser, LIDAR, ALS)
   3. Full-waveform LIDAR: History and background, applications, wave form processing
4. **Visualization of ALS Data** (4 hours)
   1. Visualization of laser scanner data: Imaging height data (Imaging point clouds, shading, coloring, perspective views); Imaging reflectance data; Point cloud viewing
   2. Visualization for error analysis: Visual inspection (Data completeness, systematic height errors, scanner artifacts, systematic reflectance errors, quality of filtering, quality of extracted features); Measurements in strip overlap; Comparison to reference height data; Comparison to maps
5. **Mobile and Terrestrial Laser Scanning (MLS and TLS)** (4 hours)
   1. Principles
   2. Properties of TLS/MLS
   3. Point cloud properties
   4. Devices and platforms
   5. MLS and TLS registration
   6. Applications
6. **Segmentation of Point Clouds** (3 hours)
   1. Extraction of information from point clouds
   2. Segmentation algorithms
   3. Extraction of smooth surfaces: Scan line segmentation, surface growing, surface merging, voxel space analysis
   4. Extraction of parameterized surfaces: Planes, cylinders, spheres
7. **Applications of LIDAR** (5 hours)
   1. Digital elevation model
   2. Forestry: Laser interaction with tree canopies; Forestry measurements obtainable with LiDAR; Forest inventory parameters; Biomass / carbon parameters; Leaf-on versus leaf-off; Change detection for forestry applications
   3. Building extraction and reconstruction
   4. Natural hazards: Flood; Earthquakes; Soil erosion and landslide
8. **Radar Imaging Technology** (8 hours)
   1. Transmitting and receiving polarized radiations
   2. Radar imaging system: Pulse compression radar; Range resolution and resolution along track direction; Synthetic aperture radar (SAR) and its governing equation; Swath width and bounds on pulse repetition frequency; The radar resolution cell; Squint and spotlight operating mode
   3. Radar target interaction: Radar equation; Radar cross section (Theoretical expression, cross section in dB); Distributed targets, scattering coefficient in dB and polarization dependence; Scattering matrix; Target vectors; Covariance and coherency matrices; Measuring the scattering matrix and relating to stokes vector; Polarization synthesis and compact polarimetry
9. **Radar Image Interpretation** (8 hours)
   1. Analytical complexity
   2. Visual Interpretation: Role of incidence angle, wavelength and polarization
   3. Quantitative analysis of radar image data: Overviews of methods; Features available for radar quantitative analysis; Application of standard classification techniques; Classification based on radar Image statistics (Maximum likelihood approach; Handling multi-look data; Relating scattering and covariance matrices; Stokes scattering operator)
   4. Interpretation based on structural models: Polarization phase difference; Structural decomposition (Scattering matrix, Freeman-Durden approach, Cloude-Pottier approach)

## Practicals

1. Visualization and error analysis of ALS, MLS and TLS Data
2. Building facades extraction
3. Forest inventory parameters estimation
4. Post-disaster damage assessment i.e., flood, earthquake
5. Radar image visualization
6. Radar image classifications
