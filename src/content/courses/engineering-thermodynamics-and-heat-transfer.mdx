## Syllabus

1. **Basic Concepts** (4 hours)
   1. Definition and scope of engineering thermodynamics
   2. Value of energy to society
   3. Microscopic versus macroscopic viewpoint
   4. Concepts and definitions: system, surroundings, boundary and universe; closed, open and isolated systems; thermodynamic properties (intensive, extensive and specific); thermodynamic equilibrium; thermodynamic state; thermodynamic process, cyclic process, quasi-equilibrium process, reversible and irreversible process
   5. Specific volume, pressure and pressure measurement devices, temperature and temperature measurement
   6. Zeroth law of thermodynamics, equality of temperature
2. **Energy and Energy Transfer** (3 hours)
   1. Energy and its meaning
   2. Stored energy and transient energy; total energy
   3. Energy transfer: heat transfer, work transfer
   4. Expressions for displacement work transfer
   5. Other examples of work: electrical work and mechanical forms of work
3. **Properties of Common Substances** (6 hours)
   1. Pure substance and state postulate
   2. Ideal gas and ideal gas relations
   3. Two phase (liquid and vapor) systems: phase change processes (T-v, P-v and P-T diagrams); subcooled liquid, saturated liquid, wet mixture, critical point, quality, moisture content, saturated vapor and superheated vapor; properties of two-phase mixtures; compressibility factor
   4. Development of property data: graphical data presentation (P-h, h-s and T-s diagrams) and tabular data presentation
4. **First Law of Thermodynamics** (6 hours)
   1. First law of thermodynamics for control mass
   2. First law for control mass undergoing cyclic process
   3. Internal energy, enthalpy and specific heats
   4. First law of thermodynamics for control volume
   5. Control volume analysis: steady state analysis
   6. Control volume steady state work and flow applications
   7. First law for an isolated system and PMM-I type
5. **Second Law of Thermodynamics** (6 hours)
   1. Necessity of formulation of second law
   2. Concepts and definitions
   3. Thermal reservoir, heat engine, heat pump, refrigerator; reversible and irreversible processes
   4. Kelvin-Planck and Clausius statements of the second law and their equivalence
   5. Carnot cycle and Carnot efficiency
   6. Clausius inequality and entropy
   7. Second law of thermodynamics for an isolated system
   8. Entropy changes of ideal gases, liquids and solids from Gibbs equation
   9. Concepts of change in entropy of control mass and control volume
   10. Isentropic processes for ideal gases, liquids and solids
   11. Isentropic efficiencies of steady state applications
6. **Gas Power Cycles** (8 hours)
   1. Classification of cycles
   2. Air standard assumptions
   3. Brayton cycle: analysis of closed and open cycles; cycle with intercooling, reheating and regeneration; gas turbine power plant layout and components
   4. Internal combustion cycles: operation of four strokes engine; air standard Otto cycle; air standard diesel cycle; Otto and diesel cycle comparison and mean effective pressure
   5. Diesel power plant: essential components, plant layout, performance of I.C. engines
7. **Vapor Power Cycles and Vapor Compression Refrigeration Cycles** (4 hours)
   1. Rankine cycle: ideal and actual cycle, reheat cycle, regenerative cycle, essential components and layout of steam power plant
   2. Vapor compression refrigeration cycle and its performance measurement
8. **Heat Transfer** (8 hours)
   1. Basic concepts and modes of heat transfer
   2. One dimensional steady state heat conduction through a plane wall
   3. Radial steady state heat conduction through a hollow cylinder
   4. Heat flow through composite structures: composite plane wall, multilayer tubes
   5. Critical radius of insulation for cylinders
   6. Electrical analogy for thermal resistance
   7. Combined heat transfers and overall heat transfer coefficient for plane and composite wall and tube
   8. Nature of convection; free and forced convection
   9. Heat transfer from extended surfaces: fins, types of fins, fin equation, fin performance, fins effectiveness, proper length of fin
   10. Heat radiation, Stefan's law, emissivity, absorptivity, reflectivity and transmissivity; black body, white body and gray body

## Practicals

1. Temperature measurement using different types of thermometers
2. Experiment related to first law of thermodynamics
3. Heat pump: coefficient of performance of heat pump/refrigerator
4. Heat conduction: investigate Fourier's law of linear heat conduction
5. Heat radiation: investigate Stefan-Boltzmann relationship
6. Effect of extended surfaces on heat transfer
