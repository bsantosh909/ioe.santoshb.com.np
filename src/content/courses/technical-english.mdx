## Syllabus

1. **Technical Proposals** (3 hours)
   1. Purpose and types of technical proposal
   2. Structure of proposal: title page, letter of transmittal, table of content, list of illustrations, executive summary/abstract; introduction (problem, solution, objective, data source, scope and limitation); discussion (methods, scheduling, materials/equipment, cost); list of references, appendix, glossary
   3. Key considerations and examples of technical proposal
2. **Research Proposals** (3 hours)
   1. Purpose and types of research proposal
   2. Structure of proposal: title page, letter of support, table of content, list of illustrations, executive summary, abbreviation; introduction (background, problem statement, objective, research hypothesis, scope and limitation); literature review; methodology (methods, materials/equipment); timeline and budget; list of references, appendices
   3. Key considerations and examples
3. **Technical Reports** (4 hours)
   1. Components of report: header, body, footer section
   2. Key technical reports (purpose, structure and key consideration): inception report, progress report, feasibility report, detailed project report, project completion report
   3. Short report
   4. Case study
4. **Manuscript for Journal** (3 hours)
   1. Difference between report and research article
   2. Journal and scientific dissemination
   3. Structure: title, authors, abstract, keywords, introduction, statement of problem, research questions, hypothesis, methodology, significance of the study, results, discussion, conclusion, declarations, references
   4. Key considerations
5. **Citation and Referencing** (2 hours)
   1. In-text citation: direct quote citations, indent citation, indirect citation, citing from books and journals, citing multiple authors in a single text, citing multiple texts from the same author
   2. Using numerical, pagination, preparing a reference page
