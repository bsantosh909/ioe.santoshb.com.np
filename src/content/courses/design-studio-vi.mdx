## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (4 hours)
   1. Nature, scope, size and scale of project
   2. Reflection on studio objectives
   3. Literature review and case selection
   4. Field research tools
2. **Literature and Site Study** (20 hours)
   1. Theories, standards and regulations for large span complex building types
   2. Case studies (secondary sources)
   3. Case documentation (field visits)
   4. Site analysis
   5. Presentation of findings
3. **Conceptualization** (15 hours)
   1. Program development (literature, field studies and user analysis)
   2. Conceptual development
   3. Concept diagrams and study models
   4. Conceptual design presentation and feedback
4. **Design Development** (90 hours)
   1. Architectural design and drawings
   2. Structural systems, spatial organization, materials and construction logic
   3. Integration of building services
   4. Drawings, models and 3D visualization
5. **Final Design** (15 hours)
   1. Drawings, models and documentation report
   2. Final presentation
6. **Time Problem** (6 hours)
   1. Short-duration design exercise to assess the ability to synthesize program, spatial organization and structural and service integration
