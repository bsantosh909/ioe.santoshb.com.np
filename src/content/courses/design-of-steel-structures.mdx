## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (4 hours)
   1. Steel structure: Scope; advantages and disadvantages; types
   2. Structural steel and classification of steel sections
   3. Design process and basis for design
   4. Method of analysis and design (Working stress method; Limit state design method; Ultimate load method)
   5. Prevailing codes and standards
2. **Connections in Steel Structures** (13 hours)
   1. Connection importance and types
   2. Welded connections: Welds, welding, simple and eccentric connections
   3. Bolted connections: Bolts, bolting, simple and eccentric connections
   4. Riveted connections: Brief introduction
3. **Tension Members** (4 hours)
   1. Definition and types of tension members
   2. Sectional area of tension members
   3. Design of simple and built-up sections
   4. Design of lug angle and tension splices
4. **Flexure Members** (10 hours)
   1. Steel beams and types
   2. Design of simple and built-up beams
   3. Design of continuous beams
   4. Plate girders: Necessity, requirements, design for bending/shear/deflection/lateral stability, curtailment, web and flanged splices
5. **Compression Members** (10 hours)
   1. Types of compression members
   2. Buckling behavior
   3. Design of simple and built-up sections
   4. Lateral bracing design
   5. Eccentrically loaded columns
   6. Column bases and splices
6. **Design of Roof Trusses** (4 hours)
   1. Types and components
   2. Loads and wind load calculations
   3. Design of roof components
