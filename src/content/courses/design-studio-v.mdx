## Syllabus

1. **Introduction** (4 hours)
   1. Studio project briefing: project components, target users, project scale, objectives
   2. Location and features of the proposed site
   3. Design processes
2. **Literature Review** (10 hours)
   1. Theoretical review
   2. Design standards
   3. Climate responsive designs
   4. Building material and technology
   5. Structural considerations
   6. Inference and comparative analysis
3. **Case Studies** (15 hours)
   1. National and international case studies
   2. Secondary international case studies
   3. Component based study
   4. Inferences from case studies from analytical perspective
4. **Site Analysis** (10 hours)
   1. Detailed study of the site features and surroundings
   2. Inferences from the site study for developing site-specific designs
5. **Program Formulation** (8 hours)
   1. Identification of design project components
   2. Formulation of detailed area and space requirements and calculations
6. **Design Concept** (15 hours)
   1. Development of design concept and design approaches
   2. Bubble diagram, zoning, conceptual drawings in 2D and 3D
7. **Design Development** (70 hours)
   1. Development of design concept into detailed design
   2. Detail site plan
   3. Block model and conceptual model
   4. Double line architectural floor plans with structural grid plan, sections, elevations, building form and 3D sketches
8. **Final Presentation and Submission** (12 hours)
   1. Submission of all detailed architecture drawings
   2. Detailed 3D model of building and site
9. **Time Problem** (6 hours)
   1. Design of one specified facility or component in a specific given time
   2. Produce architectural drawings as per the requirement of the topic given
