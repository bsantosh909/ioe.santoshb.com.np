## Syllabus

1. **Organization and Management** (6 hours)
   1. Concept and definitions
   2. Historical development
   3. Principles and structure of organization
   4. Levels of management
   5. Theories of management
2. **Human Resource Management** (4 hours)
   1. Human resource planning and selection
   2. Motivation theories
   3. Job specification and description
   4. Job evaluations and merit rating
3. **Production and Operation Management** (8 hours)
   1. Production and operation management in manufacturing and service environments
   2. Emerging trends
   3. Product development and testing: Product life cycle
   4. Product management and project evaluation techniques
   5. Production planning: Intermediate planning in perspective; Concept and overview of aggregate planning; Basic strategies for meeting uneven demand; Techniques for aggregate planning
4. **Quality and Productivity Engineering** (6 hours)
   1. Productivity and factors affecting productivity
   2. Concept of quality
   3. Total quality management (TQM)
   4. Statistical process control (SPC)
   5. Lean production and continuous improvement (Kaizen)
5. **Entrepreneurship** (4 hours)
   1. Need, scope and importance of entrepreneurship
   2. Entrepreneurial process
   3. Dynamics of entrepreneurship
6. **Business Idea and Business Plan** (9 hours)
   1. Business opportunities
   2. Generation of ideas
   3. Feasibility analysis
   4. Industry and competitor''s analysis
   5. Formulation of business plan
7. **Establishing a Firm** (4 hours)
   1. Business licenses and permit
   2. Form of business organization
   3. Financing and source of funding
   4. Team building
8. **Marketing Management and Growth Strategies** (4 hours)
   1. Marketing concept and strategy; Market segmentation and target group
   2. The 4Ps (Product, Price, Place and Promotion) of marketing
   3. Key marketing issues for new ventures
   4. Intellectual property rights
